Privacy Notice

How Zugado Technologies handles information across this corporate website.

Legal Notice & Scope: This document represents a working draft template for the public website of Zugado Technologies Private Limited. It does not govern separate terms for commercial products or operational consumer/provider platforms that may launch in the future.

1. Information We Collect

This website is designed with a static-first, minimal-data architecture. We do not require account registration or user profiling to access corporate pages.

When you voluntarily reach out via our contact, careers, or contributor channels, we collect only the personal information you supply:

  • Full name, email address, and professional affiliation
  • Links to portfolios, GitHub profiles, or LinkedIn accounts
  • Message content and professional summaries provided in inquiries

2. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics pixels, or behavioral tracking scripts on this website.

Standard HTTP server request logs (including IP address, browser user-agent, and requested file path) may be temporarily recorded by our hosting infrastructure (such as GitHub Pages or AWS CloudFront) strictly for security, rate-limiting, and network diagnostics.

3. Purpose of Processing

We process information submitted through this website exclusively for:

  • Evaluating and responding to corporate, technology, and partnership inquiries
  • Reviewing talent submissions and scheduling prospective employment discussions
  • Assessing independent contributor expressions of interest for Zugado Labs
  • Complying with statutory and legal obligations in India

4. Data Retention & Security

We retain correspondence only for the period necessary to fulfill the operational purpose of the communication or to satisfy applicable corporate recordkeeping requirements. Inquiries and resumes are retained securely and are not shared with third-party data brokers or marketing affiliates.

5. Inquiries & Data Rights

If you wish to review, update, or request the deletion of personal communications you have previously provided to Zugado Technologies, you may contact our administrative team at:

hello@zugadotechnologies.com
